# Questions, answered with data

Data-driven articles about how Americans shop, by [Suyash Sapre](https://suyashh77.github.io/suyashsapre_portfolio/). This is a static GitHub Pages site; the host needs no build step.

- [Walmart wins the weekly run. Target wins the weekend.](walmart-vs-target/index.html): Over half of Target's shoppers also shop at Walmart. The split isn't who walks in the door. It's which trip each store gets.
- [Within the same county, Home Depot and Lowe's shoppers look alike](home-depot-vs-lowes/index.html): Nationally their crowds diverge. Compare shoppers in the same county and most of that gap disappears. Home Depot still wins more trips.
- [Dunkin' peaks at 9 a.m. Starbucks peaks at noon. What does that say about their customers?](dunkin-vs-starbucks/index.html): A cheaper weekday routine and a weekend treat on different clocks, and a payment system that makes card counts an incomplete picture of the coffee run.
- [The gym rush comes in February. At Planet Fitness, it comes in June.](gyms/index.html): Where gyms cluster, which brands are opening and closing, what members pay by card, and when people actually show up, from budget chains to Life Time and Equinox.

`assets/site.css` is the one stylesheet every page uses. Pages are generated from a separate analysis repository. Only aggregates are published: no store, card, phone or person identifiers.
